# Figure A.3: the last three rows of Panel B

The sample is the Table IX estimation frame, keeping each pair's matched cohort and event year.
Every model regresses the outcome on Merged × Post with pair fixed effects and matched-pair by
calendar-year fixed effects, standard errors clustered by matched pair.

**Excluding employment separations after allegations.** Employee Disclosure (All) and Employee
Misconduct (EMS) recomputed from the adviser disclosure records without that category, on the
Table IX employee-record denominator, winsorized at the pooled 1st and 99th percentiles.

**Announcement year and closing year.** Mergers whose closing date is on or after the
announcement date and falls in 2011–2020 (231 matched pairs). The same observations are
estimated with the event dated by the announcement year and by the closing year.
