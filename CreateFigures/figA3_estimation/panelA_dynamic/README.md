# Figure A.3: Panel A and the first two rows of Panel B

The sample is the Table IX estimation frame: real and pseudo target–acquirer pairs from three
years before to three years after mergers completed between 2011 and 2020, after the Table IX
final-sample and winsorizing rule (5,506 pair-years, 423 matched pairs).

**Panel A.** Employee Disclosure (All) regressed on Merged × event-year indicators at −3, −2, 0,
+1, +2 and +3 (−1 omitted), with pair fixed effects and matched-pair by calendar-year fixed
effects, standard errors clustered by matched pair. Estimated for all matched pairs and for
the pairs in which none of the four firms takes part in another merger within three years of
the deal.

**Panel B, first two rows.** The Table IX specification (Merged × Post with the Table IX
controls and year fixed effects, clustered by matched pair) for Employee Disclosure (All) and
Employee Misconduct (EMS): once with the Table IX outcome, and once counting each adviser once
per pair-year instead of once per registration.
